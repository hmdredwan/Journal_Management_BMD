Welcome to the Bangladesh Meteorological Department Journal

The Bangladesh Meteorological Department Journal (BMD Journal) serves as a significant platform for disseminating research, data, and studies related to meteorology, climate, and environmental science in Bangladesh. As a crucial part of the country's meteorological infrastructure, the journal provides a space for the publication of articles, research papers, and technical reports that contribute to a better understanding of weather patterns, climate change, and natural disaster management. The BMD Journal not only supports national and regional meteorological advancements but also fosters collaboration among meteorologists, researchers, and climate scientists. Through this journal, the Bangladesh Meteorological Department aims to enhance public awareness and policy-making on weather-related issues and contribute to the global discourse on climate science.
